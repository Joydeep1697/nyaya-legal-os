3. Learned counsel for revisionist assails the impugned order mainly on

the ground that the learned Family Court failed to adjust the maintenance

awarded in the other proceedings. It is contended by learned counsel for

revisionist that prior to the proceedings that led to the impugned order, the

present respondent had filed proceedings under The Protection of Women

from Domestic Violence Act in which the learned Magistrate awarded

monthly maintenance at the rate of Rs.13,000/-. According to the revisionist,

while passing the impugned order, the learned Family Court ought to have

adjusted that amount of Rs. 13,000/-, which has not been done, so it is a case

of unjust enrichment.

4. Learned counsel for respondent appearing with respondent in person

on advance intimation accepts notice and on instructions in all fairness

concedes to the argument advanced on behalf of the revisionist. Learned

counsel for respondent submits that in the interest of expeditious disposal,

this petition may be disposed of with consent and the impugned order be

modified to the extent that the interim maintenance be awarded at the rate of

Rs. 17,000/- per month so far as the proceedings pending before the Family

Court are concerned.

5. Accordingly, with consent of both sides, the impugned order is

modified to the extent that the revisionist shall pay monthly interim

maintenance to the present respondent at the rate of Rs. 17,000/- from the

date of filing the maintenance petition till its disposal. However, it is made
