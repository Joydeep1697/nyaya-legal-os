HIGH COURT OF DELHI: NEW DELHI
DELHI HIGHER JUDICIAL SERVICE PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION-2026

Date: 28.07.2026
NOTICE

It is to notify all concerned that the Model Answer Keys of Question Paper
Booklets of all the Series, i.e. A, B, C and D regarding Delhi Higher Judicial Service
Preliminary Examination, 2026 held on 26.07.2026 have been prepared and the same are
attached herewith.

If any candidate has any objection regarding the aforesaid Model Answer Keys,
he/she may file his/her objection(s) through online mode ONLY on the link provided on
the official website of this Court within 03 days from the date of this Notice, i.e.,
31.07.2026 (upto 05.00 PM). Objections received thereafter shall not be entertained.
Any representation regarding objections received by any other mode will not be
entertained. It is clarified that the candidates are restricted from choosing the same
answer option as given in the Model Answer Keys while raising the Objection. It is
mandatory for candidates to give justification/reasons in support of their objection(s).
