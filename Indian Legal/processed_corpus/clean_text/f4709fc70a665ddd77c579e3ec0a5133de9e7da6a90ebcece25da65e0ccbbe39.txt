HIGH COURT OF DELHI: NEW DELHI

No. 34 /D-3/Gaz.IA/DHC/2026
Dated, 04th August, 2026

ORDER

The footnote nos. (ii), (iii) and (vii), of this Court's Order No. 32//D-3/Gaz.IA/DHC/2026
dated O1.08.2026, be read as:

(ii) The Court of ASJ-03, New Delhi (Presided over by Sh. Prashant Sharma),
Court of ASJ-02, New Delhi (Presided over by Sh. Amit Bansal) and the court of
Special Judge, NDPS, New Delhi (Presided over by Sh. Jitender Pratap Singh)
shall stand abolished. The Courts of ASJ-04 to ASJ-07, New Delhi, shall stand
re-numbered as ASJ-02 to ASJ-05, respectively.

(iii) POCSO courts of New Delhi Districts which are currently working from
RACC shall be shifted back to PHC, New Delhi and shall henceforth start
functioning from Patiala House Courts. The Courts of ASJ (POCSO)-02 and ASJ
(POCSO)-03, New Delhi, shall stand re-numbered as ASJ (POCSO)-06 and ASJ
(POCSO)-07, New Delhi, respectively.

(vii) All the cases under MCOCA Act as well as other connected cases presently
being tried by different Sessions Courts of all districts of Delhi shall stand
transferred and assigned by the Ld. PDJ, RACC to the newly created courts of
SEC-11 to SEC-13 at RACC while ensuring equal distribution. However, the
MCOCA Cases being investigated by the Special Cell, Delhi Police shall be
assigned to the SEC- I I only.

BY ORDER OF THE COURT
