New Delhi, the 25th July, 20191 Shravana 3, 1941 (Saka)

The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the
24th July, 2019, and is hereby published for general information:—

THE NATIONAL INVESTIGATION AGENCY (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2019

No. 16 OF 2019
[24th July, 2019.]
An Act to amend the National Investigation Agency Act, 2008.

BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventieth Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1. (1) This Act may be called the National Investigation Agency (Amendment) Short title and
Act, 2019. commencement.
(2) It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification
in the Official Gazette, appoint.
