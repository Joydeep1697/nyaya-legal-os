The following Act of Parliament received the assent of the President on the
23rd September, 2011, and is hereby published for general information.
THE CONSTITUTION (NINETY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 2011
(23rd September, 2011.]
AnAct further to amend the Constitution of India.
Ber it enacted by Parliament in the Sixty-second Year of the Republic of India as
follows:—
1. This Act may be called the Constitution (Ninety-sixth Amendment) Act, 2011. Short title.
2. Inthe Eighth Schedule to the Constitution, in entry 15, for the word “Oriya”, the Amendment
word “Odia” shall be substituted. of Eighth
