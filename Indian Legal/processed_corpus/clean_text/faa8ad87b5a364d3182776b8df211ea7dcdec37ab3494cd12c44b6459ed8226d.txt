Note(s):

(i) The Judicial Officers shall be under the control of the Principal District & Sessions Judge of
the district to which they have been allocated.

(ii) The Court of ASJ-03, New Delhi (Presided over by Sh.Prashant Sharma), Court of ASJ-02,
New Delhi (Presided over by Sh.Amit Bansal) and the court of Special Judge, NDPS, New Delhi
(Presided over by Sh.Jitender Pratap Singh) shall stand abolished.

[PAGE 11]
(iii) POCSO courts of New Delhi Districts which are currently working from RACC shall be
shifted back to PHC, New Delhi and shall henceforth start functioning from Patiala House
Courts.

(iv) Cases under NIA Act, 2008 investigated by National Investigating Agency (NlA) as well as
other connected cases which are at present being tried by PD&SJ, NDD, PHC and ASJ-03, NDD,
PHC shall stand transferred to the new NIA courts i.e. SEC-01 to SEC-03, RACC in equal
proportion. Remaining cases in the court of ASJ-03, NDD, PHC, New Delhi, if any, be assigned
to other Sessions Courts by PDJ, NDD, PHC, New Delhi.

(v) The cases under UAPA (Scheduled Offences under NIA Act) investigated by State Agencies
as well as other connected cases which are being tried at present by ASJ-02, NDD, PHC, New
Delhi shall stand transferred to the courts of SEC-04 & SEC-05 at RACC in equal proportion. All
cases of other Scheduled Offences (other than UAPA) under NIA Act investigated by State
Agencies pending in the court of ASJ-02, NDD, PHC, New Delhi shall stand transferred to SEC-
06. Remaining cases in the court of ASJ-02, NDD, PHC, New Delhi, if any, be assigned to other
Sessions Courts by PDJ, NDD, PHC, New Delhi.

(vi) The cases under NDPS Act pending in the court of Special Judge, NDPS, New Delhi which
are investigated by Central Agencies viz., NCB, CNB shall be assigned by PDJ, New Delhi
